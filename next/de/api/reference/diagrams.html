Diagrams ​

Operationen des Tags Diagrams, die dieses Release implementiert. Erzeugt aus api/openapi.yaml; geplante Operationen sind nicht aufgeführt. Die Beschreibungen stammen aus dem englischen API-Vertrag.

List diagrams (US-074, UC-10, SCR-067) ​

GET /diagrams · listDiagrams

The diagrams that are not deleted, ordered by name (case-insensitive), then by ID, in pages of 1 to 200 (default 50). q keeps the diagrams whose name contains it, case-insensitively and literally (% and _ are no wildcards). A pageSize outside 1-200, a page below 0, a q over 200 characters, and a cursor of another query, a changed cursor or one without page answer 400, the last naming cursor. Read only, not audited.

Zugriff: Berechtigung entity:read

Create a diagram (US-074, UC-10 step 1) ​

POST /diagrams · createDiagram

Creates an empty diagram with version 1 for an existing viewpoint. The name is trimmed, has 1 to 200 characters and is unique among the diagrams, ignoring case (urn:oea:problem:diagram-name-taken, 422 naming name); the description has at most 2000 characters. An unknown or missing viewpoint, a blank or over-long name and an over-long description answer 422 naming the field. Persons and service accounts with entity:write create diagrams and are their creator; the local system admin account has no person and gets 403. Writes the audit event diagram.created.

Zugriff: Berechtigung entity:write

Change the viewpoint of a diagram (US-074, UC-10) ​

PUT /diagrams/{id}/viewpoint · changeDiagramViewpoint

Changes the viewpoint of the diagram to another existing one (owner decision 2026-11-19); it is saved at once. Needs If-Match with the version read (428 without, 412 when stale; the version is shared with updateDiagram and saveDiagramView). Shapes, edges and hierarchy lines stay as they are; the shown shapes and edges whose type the new viewpoint does not allow become conflicts (conflictCount, computed when the view is read) and block saveDiagramView until they are resolved. The same viewpoint answers 200 with the unchanged diagram: no new version, no audit. Otherwise the version is raised and diagram.viewpoint-changed is audited with fromViewpointId, toViewpointId and conflicts. An unknown viewpoint answers 422 naming viewpointId; an unknown or deleted diagram 404. The principal rules of createDiagram apply (403 for the local system admin account). Order of the answers: 400, 403, 404, 428, 412, 422.

Diagram editor data (SCR-021) ​

GET /views/diagrams/{id} · getDiagramView

The diagram with its version, its viewpoint (allowed metatypes, effective default sizes in pt), its shapes in drawing order with shape ID, entity, position and size in pt and the mandatory properties that are missing, and its edges with the connection, the source and target shape and the bend points. Shapes of a deleted entity and edges of a deleted connection or of such a shape are left out. A shape may lie in a container (parentShapeId), the view carries the hierarchy lines whose hierarchy holds and the effective setting askNewParent. Each shape and edge carries conflict and the view conflictCount (shapes and edges whose type the viewpoint does not allow). The ETag is the version of the diagram. An unknown or deleted diagram answers 404. Read only, not audited.

Save the layout of a diagram (US-074, SCR-021) ​

PUT /views/diagrams/{id} · saveDiagramView

Saves the whole layout in one request: all shapes in drawing order and the new entities of the canvas. The save is atomic. The new entities are created with the checks and the audit of an entity write (entity.created, as in the batch); a mandatory property without default value does not block them, the entity is created incomplete (owner decision 2026-11-20) and its shape reports missingMandatory. The shapes replace the stored ones; stored shapes of deleted entities that the request cannot contain are kept. Needs If-Match with the version read (428 without, 412 when stale; the version is shared with updateDiagram). Every accepted save raises the version and audits diagram.layout-saved with the numbers of added, changed and removed shapes and of created entities; a rejected save changes and audits nothing. A shape needs exactly one of entityId (an existing, active element entity) and newEntityKey; an entity of a shape that is new or shows another entity needs a metatype the viewpoint allows. Positions are within ±100 000 pt, sizes 1 to 10 000 pt, both with at most two decimals (400 otherwise). At most 2000 shapes and 500 new entities. The 422 names the shapes, edges, new entities and new connections with their reasons. X-Agent-Id and X-Agent-Run are informational (ADR-033 §7).

The save also writes the changes of shown entities (entityUpdates, at most 500: entity, expected version, optionally name, description and values) and the description and values of the new entities, in the same transaction, with the checks and the audit of a batch update (entity.updated): metamodel and property visibility (403 for the whole save), mandatory values required for a changed entity (422 MANDATORY_MISSING), history. A change names an entity that a shape of the same request shows, once (422 otherwise). A changed entity with an open issue of a solution answers 409 entity-blocked; an entity whose current version differs from expectedVersion answers 409 entity-version-conflict naming every such entity with its current version. Only the first applies, in this order: 400, 412 or 428, 422 of the layout (with the structural 422 of nesting, lines and parent changes), 403, 422 of a deleted entity (also of a parent change), 409 entity-version-conflict, 409 entity-blocked, 422 of the changes, then 403 and 422 of the new entities, 422 of the parents and 422 of the hierarchy lines. Name at most 255 and description at most 2000 characters (400 otherwise). The batch API stays without a version check. A save of only entity changes also raises the version of the diagram.

The save also carries the edges (edges, at most 4000, each with at most 50 bend points within ±100 000 pt and two decimals) and the new connections (newConnections, at most 500). An edge shows an existing connection that is not deleted or a new one, from a shape of its source entity to a shape of its target entity; a new or changed edge needs a connection metatype the viewpoint allows, a stored edge that keeps its connection stays. A new connection takes its endpoints from the shapes of its first edge and is created after the new entities, with the checks and the audit of a batch connection (entity.created, endpoint codes SOURCE_NOT_ALLOWED, TARGET_NOT_ALLOWED, name "Source → Target" when blank), incomplete when a mandatory value is missing. A new connection of a type used as scopingConnectionType needs visibility-scope:assign (403 for the whole save, before 412 or 428 and every 422). Edges of removed shapes are removed with them; edges of a deleted connection are kept and hidden. entityUpdates may name a connection that an edge of the request shows. diagram.layout-saved also carries edgesAdded, edgesChanged, edgesRemoved and connectionsCreated. The order of the answers: 400, 403 of a scoping connection type, 412 or 428, 422 of the layout and the edges, then as above, and last 403 and 422 of the new connections. The body is limited to 4 MB (413).

Collapsed sub-processes (change us-074-bpmn-pools-and-lanes): collapsed true on a shape whose entity metatype the viewpoint does not map to bpmn:SubProcess is a 422 COLLAPSED_NOT_SUB_PROCESS naming shapes[i].collapsed, with the other 422 of the layout; the same for orientation vertical on a shape whose entity metatype is not mapped to bpmn:Pool (ORIENTATION_NOT_POOL, naming shapes[i].orientation).

Nesting, lines and parents (change us-074-diagram-nesting): a shape may name a container (parentShapeId), hierarchyLines (at most 4000) replace the stored lines, and parentChanges (at most 500, each entity once, an entity that a shape of the request shows) change parents in the same transaction with the rules, the automatic composition connection and the audit entity.parent-changed of setEntityParent; a new entity may name its parent (parentEntityId or parentNewEntityKey) and then ends at version 2. Nesting alone does not change the repository. The structural 422 comes with the layout and the edges: an unknown, later or own container, a depth over 10, a cycle, a shape not completely within its container, a line between two equal shapes or a pair twice, a parent change of an entity no shape shows, twice, with both parent fields or of a connection. A parent change of a deleted entity is the 422 of a deleted entity; the 409 entity-version-conflict and entity-blocked count entity updates and parent changes together and name each conflicting entity once. After the 422 of the changes come the 422 of the parents (unknown, deleted or connection parent, a cycle, more than 10 levels, naming parentChanges[i].parentEntityId or newEntities[i].parentEntityId) and the 422 of the hierarchy lines (the child's parent is not the entity of the container after the parent changes, naming hierarchyLines[i]), and last, as above, 403 and 422 of the new connections. diagram.layout-saved also carries parentsChanged, the entities whose parent the save changed, new entities with a parent included.

Conflicts (change us-074-diagram-viewpoint-change): a save whose layout still contains a shape that keeps its entity or an edge that keeps its connection whose type the viewpoint does not allow answers 409 diagram-conflicts naming each as shapes[i] or edges[i], whatever else it changes (also a save without any change, of only entityUpdates or of only parentChanges); nothing is changed and nothing is audited. A save that removes all conflicts is accepted. New or changed shapes and edges of a type not allowed keep their 422 METATYPE_NOT_ALLOWED. The 409 diagram-conflicts comes after the 422 of the layout and the edges (with the structural 422 of nesting, lines and parent changes) and before the 403 of the property visibility and every other answer listed above. Hidden shapes and edges of deleted entities are not checked.

Assignments (change us-075-lane-assignments): a new connection that no edge shows (sourceShapeId, targetEntityId or targetNewEntityKey) is accepted for the belongs-to, performs-role and responsible types of the organization model; a new entity that no shape shows is accepted as the target of such a connection (422 NEW_ENTITY_UNUSED otherwise); removedConnections (at most 500, 400 above) deletes assignment connections softly in the same transaction with the checks and the audit of deleteEntity (entity.deleted, 409 entity-version-conflict, 409 entity-blocked). A non-empty removedConnections needs entity:delete (403 for the whole save, with the 403 of a scoping connection type and before 412 or 428 and every 422). The 422 issue codes of assignments are ORG_UNIT_LIMIT (a lane or pool shape that a new belongs-to connection leaves with more than one organizational unit, naming shapes[i]; checked in the diagram save only), NOT_AN_ASSIGNMENT, SOURCE_NOT_ON_DIAGRAM, REMOVED_CONNECTION_SHOWN and CONNECTION_NOT_FOUND (naming removedConnections[i]). The removals are written after the entities and parents, before the new connections.

Zugriff: Berechtigung entity:write; zusätzlich visibility-scope:assign, wenn: a new connection of a type used as scopingConnectionType is written; zusätzlich entity:delete, wenn: removedConnections is not empty

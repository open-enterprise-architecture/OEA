Plateau overview (US-085, SCR-065) ​

GET /plateaus · listPlateaus

All plateaus: the baseline first, then the transition and target plateaus by validFrom (empty last), then by name (case-insensitive), then the realized ones newest first (REQ-085).

Preview of the go-live of a plateau (US-086, US-087, US-157, SCR-066) ​

GET /plateaus/{id}/go-live · previewPlateauGoLive

What the go-live would do: the plateau becomes the baseline and the current baseline is realized; the active solutions of the plateau go live in the order of the last change of their deltas, each with its counts and its field conflicts at its turn; the metamodel violations that become open issues; and the warnings, which do not block. Values the caller may not read are redacted. Changes nothing.

Access: permission plateau:go-live

Take a plateau live (US-086, US-088, US-157, UC-11) ​

POST /plateaus/{id}/go-live · goLivePlateau

In one transaction: every active solution of the plateau goes live as goLiveSolution does, in the order of the preview; the plateau becomes the baseline from today (UTC); the current baseline is realized until today and read-only; other plateaus that succeeded it succeed the new baseline, and their solutions start from it. Writes plateau.went-live, one solution.went-live per solution and plateau.updated per plateau that follows. The go-live does not check solution:write or the write roles of the values. Nothing changes on a rejection or a failure (US-088).

Access: permission plateau:go-live
